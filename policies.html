Shipping and delivery

We deliver across India by courier. The shipping charge for your PIN code is shown in the cart before you place the order.

Orders are dispatched within 2 to 3 working days after your payment is confirmed. Delivery usually takes a further 3 to 8 working days depending on your location. We send the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp, and you can check your order status on this site under “Track an order” in the cart.

Payment

We accept payment by UPI. After you place an order, send it to us on WhatsApp and we reply with a UPI QR code for the exact order amount. Your order is confirmed only after the payment has reached our account.

We do not offer cash on delivery at present.

Stay safe: our QR codes are sent only from our official WhatsApp number shown on this site. You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money, and we will never ask for an OTP, card number or UPI PIN.

Bulk and customised orders are confirmed manually: 50% advance at confirmation and the remaining 50% before dispatch.

Cancellation

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched by messaging us on WhatsApp with your order number. If you have already paid, we refund the full amount. Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled.

Orders that remain unpaid for 48 hours may be cancelled by us.

Personalised and bulk orders cannot be cancelled once production has started. confirm this is your rule

Returns and damaged items

If your order arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the parcel and the product. We will send a replacement or refund you.

For hygiene and safety reasons we cannot accept returns of candles that have been lit, or of personalised items, unless they arrived damaged or incorrect. confirm this is your rule

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent to the UPI account you paid from within 5 to 7 working days of approval.

Privacy

What we collect. When you order or enquire we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, email address if you give one, what you ordered, and any message you send us. To prevent misuse we also record the network (IP) address an order or enquiry was sent from.

Why. Only to process and deliver your order, respond to your enquiry, keep the records the law requires, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your information and we do not send promotional messages unless you have agreed to receive them.

Who sees it. Our team, the courier that delivers your parcel (name, address, mobile number), and the service providers that run this site for us: Supabase (our order database), GitHub (website hosting), Google Fonts, and WhatsApp when you message us.

On your device. This site keeps your cart, saved items and recent order numbers in your browser’s storage so they are there when you return. They stay on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long. We keep order records for period, as advised by your accountant, and enquiries for period.

Your choices. You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it where we are not required by law to keep it. Contact our grievance officer: name, Support@qcare.in, +91 79776 80290. We aim to reply within 7 days.
