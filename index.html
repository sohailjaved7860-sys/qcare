Candle care

Burn it safely

  • Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each burn.
  • Place the jar on a level, heat-resistant surface, away from drafts and anything flammable.
  • Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep it away from children and pets.
  • Let the wax cool fully before moving the jar, and put the lid back on to keep the scent fresh.
